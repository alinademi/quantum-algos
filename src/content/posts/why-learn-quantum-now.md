## The hardware got serious

In December 2024, Google showed that adding qubits to its Willow chip made errors go down, not up. That is the key step toward error correction, and every major lab is now chasing it.

## Encryption is already changing

In August 2024, NIST published its first post-quantum encryption standards. Banks, browsers and governments are moving to them now, because data stolen today could be decrypted later by a large quantum computer.

> Quantum computing is no longer only a physics topic. It is a software and security topic too.

## You don't need a physics degree
