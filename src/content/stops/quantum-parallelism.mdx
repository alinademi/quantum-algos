<Depth level="kid">

A secret box acts on a mix of many possible questions. The answers are part of a shared wave. When you read it, you get just one pair.

</Depth>

<Depth level="student">

With two input qubits in |+⟩ and an output in 0, the oracle maps all four input amplitudes into corresponding input-output pairs. The lab lists the classical truth table only for comparison, not as information extracted by a quantum measurement.

</Depth>

<Depth level="engineer">

For a uniform input on n qubits, $U_f 2^{-n/2}\sum_x|x,0\rangle=2^{-n/2}\sum_x|x,f(x)\rangle$. Measuring in the computational basis yields each pair with probability $2^{-n}$. A query advantage requires operations that turn structure in amplitudes into measurable information; it does not follow solely from having exponentially many basis amplitudes.

</Depth>
