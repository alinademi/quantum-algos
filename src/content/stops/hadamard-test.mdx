<Depth level="kid">

A helper qubit compares two paths: one in which an operation happened and one in which it did not. Interference tells you how those paths overlap.

</Depth>

<Depth level="student">

Prepare a helper in 0, apply H, apply U controlled by the helper, then H and measure the helper. Label 0 as +1 and 1 as −1. To estimate the imaginary part instead, insert S† before the final H.

</Depth>

<Depth level="engineer">

Let $z=\langle\psi|U|\psi\rangle$. After controlled U the state is $(|0\rangle|\psi\rangle+|1\rangle U|\psi\rangle)/\sqrt2$. The helper has X expectation $\operatorname{Re}z$ and Y expectation $\operatorname{Im}z$. Measurement after H or after S† then H reports these, respectively. $p(0)=(1+E)/2$, where E is the selected component. Controlled U is an assumed available operation.

</Depth>
