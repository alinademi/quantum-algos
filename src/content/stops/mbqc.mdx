<Depth level="kid">

Make a connected piece of quantum material first. Asking questions of its parts moves the encoded information through it. Random answers tell you which correction to make.

</Depth>

<Depth level="student">

Entangle an input with a |+⟩ helper using CZ, then measure the input in an angled basis. The helper now carries a rotated input, possibly with an X correction. The input wire itself is consumed. Larger cluster-state patterns can implement universal circuits.

</Depth>

<Depth level="engineer">

For measurement basis $|\pm_\alpha\rangle=(|0\rangle\pm e^{i\alpha}|1\rangle)/\sqrt2$, the two-node cluster gives output $X^m H R_Z(-\alpha)|\psi\rangle$ up to global phase, with equiprobable outcomes m. Measuring via $R_Z(-\alpha)$ then H realizes this basis. Adaptive measurement angles and byproduct tracking enable universal computation on suitable cluster states ([Raussendorf and Briegel](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.86.5188)).

</Depth>
