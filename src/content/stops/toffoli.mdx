<Depth level="kid">

Two switches must both be on before the third bit flips. All three wires remain available; flip again to undo the operation.

</Depth>

<Depth level="student">

Its action is (a,b,t) → (a,b,t XOR (a AND b)). A target initially in 0 stores the AND without deleting either original bit. The other reversible example in this lab swaps two wires using three CNOTs.

</Depth>

<Depth level="engineer">

$CCX|a,b,t\rangle=|a,b,t\oplus ab\rangle$ and $CCX^2=I$. Toffoli acts as a permutation matrix, so its linear extension also works on superpositions. With fixed constants it generates reversible classical computations, including NAND with target 1. No quantum phase or arbitrary superposition is generated from basis states by Toffoli alone.

</Depth>
