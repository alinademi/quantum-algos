<Depth level="kid">

Two waves can have equal strength but different timing. Shift one wave, then combine them: a different pattern appears.

</Depth>

<Depth level="student">

Z gives a phase of 180 degrees, S gives 90 degrees and T gives 45 degrees to the |1⟩ amplitude. A subsequent H tests the relative phase. On |+⟩, Z then H gives 1 with certainty.

</Depth>

<Depth level="engineer">

The phase gate is $P(\phi)=\operatorname{diag}(1,e^{i\phi})$. $Z=P(\pi)$, $S=P(\pi/2)$, $T=P(\pi/4)$. $P(\phi)=e^{i\phi/2}R_Z(\phi)$. On $|+\rangle$, measuring after $HP(\phi)$ gives $p(0)=\cos^2(\phi/2)$ and $p(1)=\sin^2(\phi/2)$. The phase relating P and RZ is global for an unconditional gate but can matter when the operation is controlled.

</Depth>
