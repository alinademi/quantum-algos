<Depth level="kid">

Two wave shapes enter a test. A helper compares the two possibilities of swapping them or leaving them in place. The result is more often 0 when the shapes are similar.

</Depth>

<Depth level="student">

Prepare a helper in 0, apply H, controlled-SWAP of the two states, then H on the helper. Identical pure states give 0 every time. Orthogonal ones give 0 and 1 equally often.

</Depth>

<Depth level="engineer">

For input $|\psi\rangle|\phi\rangle$, the symmetric and antisymmetric components have weights $p(0)=(1+|\langle\psi|\phi\rangle|^2)/2$ and $p(1)=(1-|\langle\psi|\phi\rangle|^2)/2$. The helper Z expectation is the squared overlap. For independent mixed states the same test estimates $\operatorname{Tr}(\rho\sigma)$, not generally their Uhlmann fidelity.

</Depth>
