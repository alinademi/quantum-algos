<Depth level="kid">

Two sets of instructions can do the same job. If two flips cancel, remove them. But test the replacement on more than just one input.

</Depth>

<Depth level="student">

H X H equals Z. Two CNOTs with identical wires cancel. A CZ gate is symmetric in its two wires. Hadamards on both wires before and after a CNOT reverse the direction of control.

</Depth>

<Depth level="engineer">

Use $HXH=Z$, $H^2=I$, $CX^2=I$. In two-qubit notation, $(H\otimes H)CX_{0,1}(H\otimes H)=CX_{1,0}$. Equality of all matrix entries establishes equality on arbitrary superpositions and entangled inputs. Equality only up to a global phase needs care if a subcircuit is controlled.

</Depth>
