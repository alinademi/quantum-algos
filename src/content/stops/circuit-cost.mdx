<Depth level="kid">

Two workers can finish independent tasks at the same time. On quantum wires, two gates that do not use the same wires can often share a time step.

</Depth>

<Depth level="student">

Width tells how many qubits are allocated. Gate count counts operations. Depth counts layers when operations on disjoint wires can run together. A specified gate basis may also motivate counting CNOT or T gates separately.

</Depth>

<Depth level="engineer">

For a fixed ordered list of nonempty wire sets, assign each gate the earliest layer later than the last layer on any of its wires. The maximum assigned layer is the depth for that dependency ordering and unit-duration model. Commutation-based rewrites can change this value. Hardware duration, connectivity, measurement, classical latency and error are separate costs.

</Depth>
