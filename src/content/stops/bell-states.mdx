<Depth level="kid">

Prepare a pair of qubits with linked outcomes. You cannot tell which answer one qubit will give by itself, but the pair has a well-defined joint state.

</Depth>

<Depth level="student">

Apply H on the first bit then CNOT. Starting with each of 00, 01, 10 and 11 makes a different Bell state. Reverse the preparation to tell all four apart in a Bell-basis measurement.

</Depth>

<Depth level="engineer">

For input $|a,b\rangle$, the circuit produces $(|0,b\rangle+(-1)^a|1,1\oplus b\rangle)/\sqrt2$. These are $\Phi^\pm$ for $b=0$, $\Psi^\pm$ for $b=1$. Each partial trace is $I/2$. The inverse, CNOT then H, maps the Bell basis to the computational basis. Merely measuring in Z cannot distinguish the two signs.

</Depth>
