<Depth level="kid">

A few types of building blocks can make many different machines. Quantum gates work similarly, although a finite set may need many small steps to approximate a desired turn.

</Depth>

<Depth level="student">

Arbitrary single-qubit rotations together with CNOT are exactly universal for finite registers up to global phase. A finite set such as H, T and CNOT is approximately universal. The lab demonstrates exact examples, not a compiler for arbitrary gates.

</Depth>

<Depth level="engineer">

Single-qubit gates plus CNOT can synthesize any finite-dimensional qubit unitary up to global phase ([Barenco et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9503016)). H, T and CNOT generate a dense family up to global phase. One example is $R_Y(\pi/2)R_Z(\pi)=-iH$; another is CZ from conjugating CNOT by H on the target. Universality does not promise short circuits for every unitary.

</Depth>
