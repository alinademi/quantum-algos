Write a letter above each wire to say what to do there. The whole row of letters represents one operation on all wires.

</Depth>

<Depth level="student">

XZ means X on the first qubit and Z on the second. At a single site, two different nonidentity Pauli matrices anticommute. When two strings are multiplied, their local sign changes multiply. A pair of sign changes cancels.

</Depth>

<Depth level="engineer">

The $4^n$ strings in $\{I,X,Y,Z\}^{\otimes n}$ satisfy $\operatorname{Tr}(PQ)=2^n\delta_{P,Q}$ and span the complex matrix space. For two strings, $PQ=(-1)^k QP$ with k the number of sites having different nonidentity letters. Each string is Hermitian, unitary and squares to identity. Hermitian matrices have real expansion coefficients in this basis.

</Depth>
