<Depth level="kid">

The first bit is a switch: when it is 1, the second bit flips. In a quantum circuit both paths can happen coherently until measurement.

</Depth>

<Depth level="student">

For basis inputs, 00 and 01 stay unchanged, while 10 and 11 exchange. With |+⟩ on the control and |0⟩ on the target the output is the Bell state, not two separate |+⟩ states.

</Depth>

<Depth level="engineer">

The map is $|a,b\rangle\mapsto|a,a\oplus b\rangle$. By linearity, $(|0\rangle+|1\rangle)|0\rangle/\sqrt2$ becomes $(|00\rangle+|11\rangle)/\sqrt2$. The gate is its own inverse. Control and target are ordered: swapping them generally changes the matrix. With target $|+\rangle$ the gate instead acts trivially on that product state.

</Depth>
