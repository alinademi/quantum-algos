Instead of writing large matrices, draw colored nodes and wires. Some connected nodes of the same color can be merged, adding their phase labels.

</Depth>

<Depth level="student">

A green spider with one input and one output is P(α). A red spider is its X-basis version H P(α) H. Two spiders of the same color in series fuse by adding angles. H gates change the color of a one-input one-output spider. Diagrams can also have multiple inputs and outputs.

</Depth>

<Depth level="engineer">

In the usual unnormalized convention a Z spider with m inputs and n outputs denotes $|0\rangle^{\otimes n}\langle0|^{\otimes m}+e^{i\alpha}|1\rangle^{\otimes n}\langle1|^{\otimes m}$. X spiders use the X basis. Spider fusion joins connected same-color spiders under its rule; color change conjugates by H on all legs. For the one-to-one examples here the equalities hold exactly, including scalars. General ZX derivations need careful treatment of disconnected scalars. See the [ZX-calculus textbook](https://zxcalculus.com/).

</Depth>
