<Depth level="kid">

A coherent switch decides which branch gets a gate. The switch itself is not read. A control in superposition can connect the two qubits.

</Depth>

<Depth level="student">

CZ multiplies |11⟩ by −1. CY flips the target with Y instead of X. Controlled phase gates give a chosen phase to |11⟩. An open control activates on 0 instead of 1.

</Depth>

<Depth level="engineer">

For control-on-one, $C(U)=|0\rangle\langle0|\otimes I+|1\rangle\langle1|\otimes U$. Control-on-zero conjugates by X on the control. $CZ=(I\otimes H)CX(I\otimes H)$ and $CY=(I\otimes S)CX(I\otimes S^\dagger)$. For CP, apply $P(\phi/2)$ to each wire, CNOT, $P(-\phi/2)$ on target, then CNOT. Comparing complete matrices checks the control phase as well.

</Depth>
