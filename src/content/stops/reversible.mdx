<Depth level="kid">

If you forget the two switches after recording AND, you cannot always recover them. Keep the inputs and write the answer into a third bit so you can undo it.

</Depth>

<Depth level="student">

AND erases distinctions if only its output is retained. Toffoli instead XORs the AND into a target while keeping inputs. This mapping is reversible even when the target begins in 1. Physically erasing information can have a thermodynamic cost.

</Depth>

<Depth level="engineer">

A reversible classical gate is a bijection of bit strings and therefore extends to a unitary permutation matrix. For an independent equiprobable bit erased isothermally into a thermal bath at T, the ideal Landauer heat bound is $k_BT\ln2$. It is not a universal energy price for each gate. Reversible logic by itself does not ensure zero dissipation in a real device.

</Depth>
