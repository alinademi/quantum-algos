<Depth level="kid">

Prepare a special helper first. After joining it to the data and reading the helper, you apply a correction based on the result. The data has received the desired operation.

</Depth>

<Depth level="student">

For T injection, prepare a helper in T|+⟩. Apply CNOT from data to helper, then measure the helper. Outcome 0 leaves T on the data. Outcome 1 leaves T† on the data up to global phase, and S fixes it.

</Depth>

<Depth level="engineer">

Write $|\psi\rangle=a|0\rangle+b|1\rangle$ and $\omega=e^{i\pi/4}$. After CNOT, projecting the magic qubit onto 0 gives $(a|0\rangle+\omega b|1\rangle)/\sqrt2$; outcome 1 gives $(\omega a|0\rangle+b|1\rangle)/\sqrt2$. Each has probability 1/2. Since $ST^\dagger=T$, feed-forward yields the target T gate in both branches, up to phase. The resource state is not created by Clifford gates alone. See [Gottesman and Chuang](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9908010).

</Depth>
