<Depth level="kid">

Imagine a lamp with two switches. An AND gate lights it only when both switches are on. An OR gate lights it when at least one is on. A NOT gate flips a single switch value.

</Depth>

<Depth level="student">

XOR outputs 1 when its two inputs differ. NAND is NOT AND. Every Boolean function can be built from NAND gates alone: NOT(a) = NAND(a,a), and AND is NAND followed by NOT. Try all four pairs in the lab.

</Depth>

<Depth level="engineer">

For bits $a,b$, AND is $ab$, XOR is addition modulo 2, and OR is $a+b-ab$ using ordinary integer arithmetic. NAND is $1-ab$. NAND is functionally complete: NOT and AND are expressible with NAND, and these generate all Boolean functions. A usual Boolean gate may map several inputs to one output; a quantum gate must instead be a reversible linear map on its full register.

</Depth>
