Choose an angle and search sequences of H, T and T†. The lab tries bounded words and measures the best distance up to global phase. A larger search bound cannot worsen the best distance, but exhaustive search grows quickly.

<Depth level="engineer">

For a fixed dimension and finite inverse-closed alphabet dense in SU(d), Solovay–Kitaev constructs approximations with word length polylogarithmic in $1/\epsilon$. The standard recursive method uses approximations and group commutators; see [Dawson and Nielsen](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0505030). For gates defined up to phase, work with appropriate phase representatives. Here the distance for two 2 by 2 unitaries is $\min_\alpha\|U-e^{i\alpha}V\|_F=\sqrt{4-2|\operatorname{Tr}(U^\dagger V)|}$. The lab is a small word search, not the recursive theorem algorithm.

</Depth>
