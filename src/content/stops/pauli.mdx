<Depth level="kid">

X flips the two bit labels. Z changes the sign of the 1 wave. Y does both with a special phase. None of these needs to measure the qubit.

</Depth>

<Depth level="student">

X maps 0 to 1 and 1 to 0. Y maps 0 to i times 1 and 1 to −i times 0. Z leaves 0 alone and multiplies 1 by −1. Try each twice: the state returns exactly.

</Depth>

<Depth level="engineer">

The matrices are $X=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$, $Y=\begin{pmatrix}0&-i\\i&0\end{pmatrix}$ and $Z=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$. Each is Hermitian and squares to $I$. $XY=iZ$, $YX=-iZ$, so different Paulis anticommute. Since $R_P(\pi)=-iP$, their action on the Bloch sphere is a rotation of 180 degrees; the global phase is not shown.

</Depth>
