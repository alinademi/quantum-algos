<Depth level="kid">

Two qubits can exchange places, exchange parts of their waves or pick up phases together. These operations are not all the same.

</Depth>

<Depth level="student">

iSWAP exchanges 01 and 10 and attaches i. Square-root SWAP squares to SWAP. XX and ZZ rotations act through products of Pauli operators. fSim mixes 01 and 10 and adds a phase to 11.

</Depth>

<Depth level="engineer">

Use the ordered basis 00, 01, 10, 11. $R_{PP}(\theta)=e^{-i\theta P\otimes P/2}$; the lab uses the two-qubit XX convention for MS. fSim has the odd-parity block $\begin{pmatrix}\cos\theta&-i\sin\theta\\-i\sin\theta&\cos\theta\end{pmatrix}$, diagonal entries 1 and $e^{-i\phi}$ on 00 and 11. ECR uses the IBM [ECRGate convention](https://quantum.cloud.ibm.com/docs/en/api/qiskit/qiskit.circuit.library.ECRGate) reordered to q0-first. Physical implementations may differ in phases and pulse conventions.

</Depth>
