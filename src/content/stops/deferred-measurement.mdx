<Depth level="kid">

Instead of reading a switch now, keep it as a quantum switch. Read it later. When nothing else needs the earlier answer, the final recorded answers can match.

</Depth>

<Depth level="student">

Measure the control in Z, flip the target if the result is 1, and read both bits. Compare this with a CNOT followed by measuring both. The probabilities agree despite different states before the final readout.

</Depth>

<Depth level="engineer">

For input $\sum_m|m\rangle|\psi_m\rangle$, coherent controlled $U_m$ gives $\sum_m|m\rangle U_m|\psi_m\rangle$. Measuring the control in the same basis yields the same branches and weights as early measurement and feed-forward. This requires preserving the relevant control record; later interference of the control can invalidate an unqualified substitution.

</Depth>
