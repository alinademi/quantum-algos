<Depth level="kid">

Imagine two boxes and a switch. Switch on: exchange the boxes. Switch off: leave them alone. The quantum version keeps both control branches coherent.

</Depth>

<Depth level="student">

For control 0, the targets do not change. For 1, their positions exchange. A control in |+⟩ with different targets produces a three-qubit entangled state.

</Depth>

<Depth level="engineer">

The controlled-SWAP matrix is $|0\rangle\langle0|\otimes I_4+|1\rangle\langle1|\otimes\mathrm{SWAP}$, so its square is identity. It also preserves the Hamming weight of every basis string, since it only permutes targets. Label control c and targets a,b. CNOT a→b, Toffoli (c,b)→a, CNOT a→b implements Fredkin; checking basis inputs proves the linear maps coincide.

</Depth>
