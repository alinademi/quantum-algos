<Depth level="kid">

A small collection of quantum operations moves the basic X, Y and Z questions around without making new kinds of questions.

</Depth>

<Depth level="student">

H exchanges X and Z and changes the sign of Y. S sends X to Y. CNOT can spread a single-wire Pauli across two wires. T sends X to a combination of X and Y, so T is not Clifford.

</Depth>

<Depth level="engineer">

The Clifford group is the normalizer of the n-qubit Pauli group: $UPU^\dagger$ is Pauli for every Pauli P. H, S and CNOT generate it up to global phase. For example $TXT^\dagger=(X+Y)/\sqrt2$ is not a Pauli. This conjugation closure enables tracking stabilizers without an exponential state vector, but Clifford gates can still create entanglement.

</Depth>
