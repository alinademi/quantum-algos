<Depth level="kid">

Use a spare work surface to hold an intermediate result. If it is already being used, you must not assume it is blank, and must return it as you found it.

</Depth>

<Depth level="student">

A clean scratch qubit starts in a known state such as |0⟩. A dirty scratch qubit may start in an arbitrary state. CNOT twice restores the helper, including its correlations with another register, whereas one CNOT need not.

</Depth>

<Depth level="engineer">

If the intended operation is $V$ on data, a clean-ancilla circuit may satisfy $U|\psi\rangle|0\rangle=V|\psi\rangle|0\rangle$. A borrowed dirty ancilla must instead satisfy the analogous identity for arbitrary helper states, also when entangled with an external system. A lone compute step can entangle scratch with data; discarding it can decohere the data.

</Depth>
