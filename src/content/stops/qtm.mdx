<Depth level="kid">

Imagine a tape reader that may move left or right in superposition. Instead of choosing one path immediately, its possible paths can interfere.

</Depth>

<Depth level="student">

A quantum configuration specifies the internal state, head position and tape symbols. Transition amplitudes can coherently connect configurations. The lab simplifies this to a head on a four-position ring with a two-state direction register and fixed tape symbols. It is a quantum walk, not a universal Turing machine.

</Depth>

<Depth level="engineer">

A QTM has configurations $|q,h,t\rangle$ and a local transition rule whose global evolution must be unitary; arbitrary normalized outgoing amplitudes alone do not ensure this. Uniform circuits and suitably defined QTMs simulate each other with polynomial overhead in standard complexity models ([Yao, 1993](https://doi.org/10.1109/SFCS.1993.366852)). This toy step is H on the internal coin followed by a reversible conditional cyclic shift.

</Depth>
