<Depth level="kid">

Your circuit might ask distant wires to interact. A device only lets neighbors talk. A compiler moves the qubits, rewrites the instructions, and finds a legal schedule.

</Depth>

<Depth level="student">

In this example a remote CNOT between the ends of a three-qubit line is implemented by SWAPs and a neighboring CNOT. Adjacent identical CNOTs can cancel. The original layout is restored by reversing the SWAPs.

</Depth>

<Depth level="engineer">

A transpiler can choose an initial logical-to-physical layout, insert routing operations, translate to a native gate set and schedule under target constraints. It may track a final permutation instead of restoring the initial layout; this lab restores it. Exact unitary preservation can be checked by column-wise comparison with global phase, whereas cost minimization is a separate problem.

</Depth>
