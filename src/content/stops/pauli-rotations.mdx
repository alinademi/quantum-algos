<Depth level="kid">

Make several wires speak the same basis language, combine their parity into one wire, turn its phase, then undo the combining steps.

</Depth>

<Depth level="student">

For a string such as XYZ, use H on X sites and S† then H on Y sites to map the desired observable into Z. Compute parity with CNOTs into one active wire, apply a Z rotation, then reverse all the preparation.

</Depth>

<Depth level="engineer">

Since $P^2=I$, $e^{-i\theta P}=\cos\theta I-i\sin\theta P$. If B maps P to a product of Z operators, compute their parity onto a chosen target with a CNOT network C. Then $B^\dagger C^\dagger R_Z(2\theta)CB=e^{-i\theta P}$. The factor of two comes from the definition of RZ. A string with k active sites uses $2(k-1)$ CNOTs in this construction.

</Depth>
