## Platform

web

## Users

Curious beginners up to developers, learning quantum computing on their own, on phone and desktop. *(inferred)* Many readers are not native English speakers.

## Product Purpose

Quantum Computing Era teaches quantum computing from A to Z: principles, algorithms and terms. Success: a visitor understands a concept by playing with it, then moves to the next stop. *(inferred)*

## Positioning

Learn by doing, not reading: every lesson runs a live simulator in the browser, a depth dial retells the same lesson for Kid, Student or Engineer, and a metro map shows how topics connect. *(inferred)*

## Operating Context

- Lessons sit on 5 lines (Physics, Gates, Algorithms, Hardware, Crypto) with 16 stops. Superposition is the first open stop.
- Lesson kit: depth dial, four ways (words, ket, matrix, circuit), guess then run, scrubber, gate golf, myth trap.
- A blog sits beside the courses.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Free. No accounts or backend yet; progress lives in localStorage.
- Stack: Astro + MDX, React islands for widgets, deployed by GitHub Actions to Cloudflare Pages.
- Mobile first. SEO, AEO and GEO matter: all depth levels stay in the HTML.
- The home page will later become a company page; courses become secondary. Sections stay self-contained.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Quantum Computing Era.
- Voice: plain, short, small words. No em-dashes.

## Evidence on Hand

Lesson and post content in `src/content/`. No testimonials, customers, user counts or press exist. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles

- Show the idea working before the math.
- One concept graph, many views.
- Same content for every depth; only the telling changes.
- Small and fast beats clever.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA on every page. Keyboard and screen reader support for every widget.
